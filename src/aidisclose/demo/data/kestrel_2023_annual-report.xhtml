Kestrel Software plc Annual Report 2023

Strategic report

Our business

Kestrel Software plc was founded in 1987 and is headquartered in Leeds. We employed approximately 1,450 people at the end of the fiscal year. Our revenue is generated primarily from subscriptions to our workflow software. We compete on the basis of price, service quality and reliability. Seasonal demand typically peaks in the fourth quarter. We operate through two reportable segments. In 2023 we launched Kestrel Copilot, a generative AI assistant that drafts workflow documents. Our search feature uses machine learning to rank documents by relevance for over 10,500 customers.

Principal risks and uncertainties

Our results of operations may fluctuate significantly from period to period. We depend on key personnel, and the loss of their services could harm our business. Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our financing costs. We are exposed to the credit risk of our counterparties. Our use of generative AI may expose us to intellectual property claims.

Financial review

Revenue increased compared with the prior year, driven by higher volumes. Operating expenses grew more slowly than revenue. Cash flows from operating activities remained positive. We believe our existing liquidity is sufficient for the next twelve months.
