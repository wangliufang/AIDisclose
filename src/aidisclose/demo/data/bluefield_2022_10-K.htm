Item 1. Business

Bluefield Agritech Corp. was founded in 1987 and is headquartered in Des Moines, Iowa. Our breeding services rely on artificial insemination ("AI") using semen from proven sires. We employed approximately 640 people at the end of the fiscal year. AI programs accounted for 64% of our service revenue.

Our revenue is generated primarily from livestock genetics and breeding services. We compete on the basis of price, service quality and reliability. Seasonal demand typically peaks in the fourth quarter. We operate through two reportable segments.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our results of operations may fluctuate significantly from period to period. We depend on key personnel, and the loss of their services could harm our business.

Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our financing costs. We are exposed to the credit risk of our counterparties.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue increased compared with the prior year, driven by higher volumes. Revenue from AI services increased 5% due to higher herd counts.

Operating expenses grew more slowly than revenue. Cash flows from operating activities remained positive. We believe our existing liquidity is sufficient for the next twelve months.
