Item 4. Information on the Company

Tasman Freight Ltd was founded in 1987 and is headquartered in Sydney. We employed approximately 4,700 people at the end of the fiscal year. Our revenue is generated primarily from container freight forwarding and customs brokerage. We compete on the basis of price, service quality and reliability. Seasonal demand typically peaks in the fourth quarter. We operate through two reportable segments. In 2024 our agentic AI dispatch system scheduled 70% of container pickups. We license large language models from an external vendor and use them to draft customs declarations.
