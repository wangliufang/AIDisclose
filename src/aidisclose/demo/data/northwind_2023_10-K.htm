Item 1. Business

Northwind Logistics Inc. was founded in 1987 and is headquartered in Columbus, Ohio. In 2023 we deployed an AI-powered routing engine across 120 distribution centers. We employed approximately 8,200 people at the end of the fiscal year. Artificial intelligence is transforming the logistics industry.

Our revenue is generated primarily from freight transportation services. We compete on the basis of price, service quality and reliability. Seasonal demand typically peaks in the fourth quarter. We operate through two reportable segments.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our results of operations may fluctuate significantly from period to period. If our AI models contain errors, our business could be adversely affected.

We depend on key personnel, and the loss of their services could harm our business. Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our financing costs. We are exposed to the credit risk of our counterparties.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue increased compared with the prior year, driven by higher volumes. Our AI-powered routing engine reduced fuel consumption by 7% compared with 2022.

Operating expenses grew more slowly than revenue. Cash flows from operating activities remained positive. We believe our existing liquidity is sufficient for the next twelve months.
