Crescent Pharma N.V. Annual Report 2023

Strategic report

Our business

Crescent Pharma N.V. was founded in 1987 and is headquartered in Leiden. We employed approximately 2,300 people at the end of the fiscal year. Our revenue is generated primarily from sales of oncology medicines. We compete on the basis of price, service quality and reliability. Seasonal demand typically peaks in the fourth quarter. We operate through two reportable segments. Our lead product is an aromatase inhibitor (AI) for hormone receptor-positive breast cancer. We have begun evaluating machine learning for target identification.

Principal risks and uncertainties

Our results of operations may fluctuate significantly from period to period. We depend on key personnel, and the loss of their services could harm our business. Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our financing costs. We are exposed to the credit risk of our counterparties.

Financial review

Revenue increased compared with the prior year, driven by higher volumes. Operating expenses grew more slowly than revenue. Cash flows from operating activities remained positive. We believe our existing liquidity is sufficient for the next twelve months.
